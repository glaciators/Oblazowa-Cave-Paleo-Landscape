# Late Pleistocene palaeolandscapes of the Obłazowa Cave archaeological site: an interactive 360° virtual tour

## Overview

This interactive virtual tour comprises a set of interlinked 360° panoramas presenting evidence-based reconstructions of the Late Pleistocene landscape surrounding Obłazowa Cave and the Białka River gorge in the central Western Carpathians, southern Poland. The panoramas form a connected virtual environment, allowing users to move between different viewpoints and reconstructed scenes.

Obłazowa Cave is internationally known for the discovery of an exceptional [mammoth-ivory boomerang](https://skfb.ly/oqVSt) associated with an Early Upper Palaeolithic occupation by modern humans - one of the oldest known objects of this type in Europe and possibly worldwide. Its modelled age of approximately 42.3–39.3 cal ka BP provides the principal archaeological context for the MIS 3 reconstruction at approximately 41 ka (41,000 years ago) presented here.

## Reconstructed time slices

The virtual tour combines a present-day drone panorama with reconstructions of three key time slices:

- **MIS 3, approximately 41 ka** - interstadial conditions associated with the Aurignacian occupation of Obłazowa Cave.
- **Last Glacial Maximum (LGM), approximately 24 ka** - maximum cold conditions and the greatest extent of the Tatra glaciers.
- **Bølling–Allerød (B-A) interstadial, approximately 13 ka** - the Late Glacial climatic optimum, associated with pronounced warming, vegetation expansion and Magdalenian occupation of the site.

## Scientific basis and interpretation

The virtual landscapes were created and rendered in [Terragen™ 4.7](https://planetside.co.uk/) (Planetside Software). Major landscape components are constrained by archaeological, geological, geomorphological, palaeoclimatic and palaeobotanical data, including spatial datasets and published evidence. Individual plants and animals, as well as weather, lighting and seasonal conditions, contain interpretative elements introduced for visualisation purposes.

## How to explore

Select a time slice or viewpoint from the scene menu. Drag to look around, use the controls to zoom, and select the interactive markers to move between viewpoints. The interactive map can also be used to select panoramas.

## Use of generative AI

AI tools were used only for technical assistance during the software-based workflow and not to generate the landscape imagery; generative AI was limited to modifying selected parts of previously rendered Magdalenian figures in the Bølling–Allerød scene, reindeer in the MIS 3 Kotelnica scene, and horses in the MIS 3 "Wild Horses" scene.
